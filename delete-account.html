Delete Your Account

Applies to AntiRecsys (Topic Mode) on Google Play and the App Store

1. Delete from inside the app (fastest)

Open AntiRecsys, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. You must be signed in to the account you want to delete. The account and the data attached to it are removed immediately.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email rajjatkumar5779@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, using the subject “Delete my AntiRecsys account”. We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your AntiRecsys account profile: name, email address, profile photo and account/user IDs
  • Your vault saves: URLs, titles, thumbnails, transcripts, AI summaries, notes and subtopics
  • Your custom AI-generated skills
  • Your Second Brain MCP connection key and URL
  • Your monthly save counter and legacy device-to-account links
  • Local vault/skills/chat caches after you sign out or uninstall the app

4. What may be kept

We may retain records that Google Play, the App Store, RevenueCat or tax law require us to keep (for example, proof of a subscription transaction). Those records are not used to recreate your account. Aggregate download/click counts that are not linked to your identity may also be retained.

5. Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your AntiRecsys account does not cancel an active Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Subscriptions or App Store → Subscriptions. Otherwise the store may continue to bill you.

6. Contact

Questions about deletion: rajjatkumar5779@gmail.com
