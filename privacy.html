Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

AntiRecsys is a mindful browsing and focus tool. You sign in with Google so your 2nd Brain, custom skills and Pro subscription follow you across devices and reinstalls. Preferences stay on your device; your vault and account profile are stored securely on our servers under your Google identity. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally using the device's storage. A cached copy lets the app work offline:

  • Your selected topics and any custom topics you create
  • The social or job platform you last used
  • Your session history (start time, duration, topic) — for your personal analytics only
  • Focus Timer preferences (duration selected)
  • Notification on/off preference
  • Whether onboarding has been completed
  • Cached vault items and custom skills for offline viewing
  • A Keystore/Keychain-backed session token so you stay signed in

3. Data We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account ID, name, email address and profile photo
  • Vault saves — URLs, titles, thumbnails, transcripts, AI summaries and notes
  • Custom skills you generate and your Second Brain MCP connection URL
  • Subscription status and trial eligibility (payment details are handled by Google Play / the App Store and are never seen by us)
  • A legacy device identifier, only to move saves created before you signed in onto your Google account

We never collect:

  • Your Google password or any other account password
  • Precise location, contacts, camera or microphone data
  • Your browsing history inside third-party apps
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data

We use Cloudflare to host the API and database, RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, Expo Updates to deliver over-the-air updates, and Google Play Services for distribution and billing.

4. Notifications

Notifications: We request notification permission solely to show you session reminders and quick-action controls after you explicitly start a Focus Dive. Notifications are never sent without your direct action. You can disable them at any time in the Settings tab or your device's Settings app.

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other sensitive device permissions.

5. Third-Party Platforms

When you tap to open a platform (e.g., YouTube, Instagram, Reddit), the app uses an official public deep link or URL to launch that platform. We do not inject content, modify feeds, or monitor your activity once you leave AntiRecsys. The third-party platform's own privacy policy governs your activity there.

6. App Tracking Transparency

AntiRecsys does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. No App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested because we do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking of any kind.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
